About

A young workshop, an old craft

Chamunda Enterprise is a wire-bending and CNC job-work workshop based in Vatva, Ahmedabad. We work in stainless steel and mild steel wire, forming it into the hooks, handles and fittings that go into kitchens, bathrooms and homes across the region.

We take on both wholesale job work for other manufacturers and traders, and small runs of finished wire products for direct retail.
